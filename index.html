Topology rule

A geometric region with at least one valid gate is active. A region with zero gates auto-fills charcoal and is excluded from Accuracy. Attached endpoints are persistent relationships: if the white object or receiving line moves, the attached line follows. Accuracy is calculated only from visible painted active pixels: white lines, the white object, outer border and charcoal regions are excluded.
