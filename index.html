Builder model

A new level begins completely blank. Region count is derived only from your geometry. Lines and playable-shape outlines are walls; their gates create connections. Prefilled shapes are static coloured obstacles and are excluded from Accuracy. Portals create virtual connections. Regions with no gate/portal connection are excluded.
